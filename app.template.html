Cross-label

Each row is one label. The strain dropdown (Sativa / Hybrid / Indica) sets the color. Type in the Custom box to replace the strain word with your own (e.g. CRUSHED-INDICA) — color still follows the dropdown. For THC: type the value and choose THC (prints THC:xx% on large, xx% on small) or NONE (prints exactly what you type). Then the strain name. Enter or ↓ adds a row, × removes one.
